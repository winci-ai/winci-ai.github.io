About Me

Hi! I'm Xi Weng, and I also go by Winci. I am a second-year PhD student in Computer Science at the National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Jin-Song Dong.

Before that, I received both my Bachelor's and Master's degrees from the School of Artificial Intelligence at Beihang University, advised by Prof. Lei Huang. My research centers on how machines can learn rich, general-purpose visual representations without labels, and how those representations power multimodal models and world models.

Research Interests
Visual Representation Learning Self-supervised, joint-embedding, DINO & JEPA
World Models Learning predictive models of the visual world
Multimodal Learning Small-scale LMMs, video understanding

Visual Representation (Self-Supervised) Learning

How can we learn rich, general-purpose visual representations for VLMs and world models? This question has been central to my Master's and PhD research. My work focuses on joint-embedding architectures, a paradigm strongly advocated by Prof. Yann LeCun, with particular interest in the DINO family of models and JEPA.
