Visual Representation (Self-Supervised) Learning

How can we learn rich, general-purpose visual representations for VLMs and world models? This question has been central to my Master's and PhD research. My work focuses on joint-embedding architectures, a paradigm strongly advocated by Prof. Yann LeCun, with particular interest in the DINO family of models and JEPA.
